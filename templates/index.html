Patient Clinical Data

Enter the patient's clinical information

i
Age is a major risk factor. Women above 40 years have a significantly higher risk of breast cancer. The older the patient, the higher the baseline risk.
i
BI-RADS is a scoring system used by radiologists to classify what they see in a breast scan. Category 1 = Nothing found. Category 3 = Probably fine. Category 4 = Suspicious. Category 5 = Highly suspicious of cancer.
i
If the patient has a close relative such as a mother, sister, or daughter who has had breast cancer, their own risk is significantly higher. This is one of the strongest risk factors.
i
Breast density describes how much fibrous tissue is in the breast. Type A is mostly fat and easiest to read. Type D is very dense, hardest to read, and carries higher cancer risk. Dense breasts can hide tumours on a mammogram.
i
Menopause is when a woman's monthly periods stop permanently, usually around age 50. Women who have gone through menopause have a higher risk of breast cancer than those who have not.
i
A biopsy is a procedure where a small sample of breast tissue is removed and tested for cancer. If the patient has had a biopsy before, even if the result was benign, it slightly increases future cancer risk.
